DaveOS Health

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of Oura data by DaveOS Health, a private, self-hosted personal health dashboard operated for its owner's personal use.

Information accessed

When the owner chooses to connect an Oura account, DaveOS Health requests the Oura Daily permission to retrieve daily activity information (such as step counts) and sleep information. The app may also hold OAuth credentials needed to maintain that authorized connection.

Purpose and processing

The information is used to display and analyze the owner's personal health and activity trends. The intended integration runs on the owner's locally managed DaveOS system; downloaded Oura records and connection credentials are not published on this public website.

Sharing and sale

DaveOS Health is not an advertising business. Oura health records are not sold, licensed to data brokers, or shared with advertisers. The public information pages are hosted using GitHub Pages, whose standard website-delivery and logging practices are governed by GitHub's own terms and privacy statement.

Storage and security

Health records and authorization credentials are intended to be stored on the owner's private, access-controlled device. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. The public pages here do not ask for passwords, health records, or OAuth tokens.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

The owner controls the locally stored data and may delete it from DaveOS. Oura access may be revoked through the owner's Oura account. Revoking authorization stops future authorized retrieval but does not itself remove previously downloaded local records; those must be deleted separately.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's intended access, storage, or sharing practices materially change.

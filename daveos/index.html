DaveOS Health

Private, personal health dashboard

DaveOS Health is a privately operated, self-hosted personal dashboard used to review health and wellness trends. Its intended Oura integration connects an authorized Oura account to the owner's local system to retrieve daily activity and sleep measurements.

DaveOS Health is not a public health-record service or medical device. The public pages at this address provide information about the private app and its data-handling terms; they do not display health readings or accept Oura credentials.

Oura integration

With the account owner's permission, the app is designed to request Oura's Daily permission to retrieve activity information (including steps) and sleep information. Access can be revoked from Oura's account controls.

DaveOS Health is an independent personal project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura.
